Index Diagnostic Glossary
MAE (Mean Absolute Error)

Represents the average structural distance between actual validation metrics and computed evaluations. Lower magnitudes emphasize elevated modeling stability.

MSE / RMSE

Mean Squared Error heavily penalizes outliers. Root Mean Squared Error matches variance transformations to the foundational targets.

R² Coefficient of Determination

Defines predictable structural variance ratios mapped inside target features. High scores reflect pristine performance fitting metrics.
